# Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Vertical | The client's industry: Retail, Finance, Travel, QSR (quick-service restaurants) or Entertainment |
| Geo | The region: US, EMEA or APAC |
| KPI | The metric the client optimizes: CTR or in-view rate |
| Impression | One ad view served |
| CTR | Click-through rate: clicks ÷ impressions |
| In-view rate | Viewable impressions ÷ impressions. A viewable impression is not a click |
| CPM | Cost per 1,000 impressions, in USD |
| Impressions bought | Budget ÷ CPM × 1,000, rounded down |
| Benchmark | A product's historical CTR and in-view rate for a vertical, pooled as a ratio of sums |
| Ratio of sums | Total clicks ÷ total impressions across line items, rather than an average of per-line rates |
| Forecast (`available_imps`) | Estimated impressions available for a product, vertical and geo |
| Confidence (`inventory_risk`) | How much to trust the forecast, 0 to 1; higher is more trustworthy |
| Usable inventory | Forecast × confidence (default rule) |
| Coverage | Usable inventory ÷ impressions bought; below 100%, the product can't take the budget alone |
| Viable alone | A product whose usable inventory covers the buy and meets any goal |
| Bundle | A plan of 2 or 3 products, each line at least $2,000 |
| Blended rate | A bundle's KPI rate, weighted by each line's impressions |
| Expected outcomes | Impressions × KPI rate: clicks, or viewable impressions |
| eCPC | Effective cost per click: CPM ÷ (1,000 × CTR) |
| vCPM | Cost per 1,000 viewable impressions: CPM ÷ in-view rate |
| Headroom | A share of usable inventory left unbooked as a safety buffer (0% by default) |
| Policy | A setting for a judgment call: the inventory rule and the selection rule. Every card records the policies it ran under |
